- **Dragon Ascent challenge pass.** A real playthrough reached Year 9 (turn ~34, two minutes in) with gold 2.1k at +243/tick, one or two waves faced, and no rival having ever acted — "very less challenge, economy grows too fast, no actions from other empires". Four separate causes, all fixed:
  - **Waves were sized against the wrong number.** `launchOffMapInvasion` clamped invader size to `getPlayerMilitary` — a raw headcount + `defense×10` — while the realm's real defence runs through `armyPower`, which multiplies by court `armyPowerMult`, level, elite tier and general martial. Every Power Draft card raised the player's defence and nothing on the invader side. Added `InvasionSpawnOptions.totalSoldiers`, which supplies an exact budget and bypasses the clamp; empire mode never passes it, so its behaviour is untouched (regression byte-identical).
  - **Waves now track the realm's *lagged field* power.** `WaveDirector.laggedDefencePower` reads `computeFieldDefencePower` (hosts + capital garrison) from `WAVE_LAG` waves ago. The lag is the design: outpacing the curve buys two genuinely easier waves, coasting lets it close — which is what makes a pick feel like it changed the outcome. New `computeFieldDefencePower` exists because summing *every* province's walls badly overstates what meets a wave at one place; sizing against the total made the first tuning pass lethal.
  - **Two structural bugs found by measurement, not by reading.** Waves accumulated — a 12-tick cadence spawns the next before a conquest host has finished marching, and four hosts were live at once by wave 5; the pressure curve is now a target for *total live threat*, deducting what is already marching and skipping the spawn entirely when the map is full. And raids reused the wave budget and its `MIN_WAVE_SOLDIERS` floor, so an early raid was as big as the wave it preceded and two stacked on the capital ended runs by turn 39; raids now have their own share, floor, and a `RAID_WAVE_CLEARANCE` window so they never overlap a wave.
  - **Rivals act on their own** — new `src/systems/ascent/RivalDirector.ts` raising tribute demands, coalitions and vassalage ultimatums through the prompt queue, plus border raids from the wave director. Deliberately not done by widening `tickThreatDirector`'s gate: it writes `pendingUltimatum`/`pendingForeignCard` and sets `isPaused` itself, which this mode's queue does not read and its UI does not render — so it would have deadlocked the run. Its helpers and dominance shape are reused. Every refusal lands on the wave curve (`ticksToWave` pulled forward, war appetite raised, an endured coalition returns as a heavier multi-host wave) rather than in a toast.
  - **Gold got somewhere to go.** A `hire-mercenaries` response option, income-pegged like `fortify`/`buyOff`, spawns a company immediately with no commander and no manpower — the treasury-to-survival converter. With that plus tribute demands, a measured run ended on ~1.5k gold instead of 14k.
  - Tuning lives entirely in `ascentConfig.ts`. `WAVE_PRESSURE_BASE` is the main dial and is well-conditioned: measured against a naive auto-player, 0.45 → survives ~83 seasons, 0.55 → ~73, 0.62 → ~65. Shipped at **0.5**; a thinking player fortifies, keeps a host home and buys off coalitions, so the real ceiling is well above that.
  - **A dead branch caught before shipping.** The vassalage gate asked for a rival at 1.8x `getPlayerMilitary`, but an off-map empire's strength is its `power` index (capped 122) scaled x10, so the strongest rival measures **0.45-0.70x** the player's military across an entire run — the branch could never fire, and tribute fired only in the opening seconds. Both ratios are now calibrated from measurement (`VASSAL_POWER_RATIO` 0.66, `TRIBUTE_POWER_RATIO` 0.45) and a long run produces all three kinds (coalition 12, tribute 6, vassalage 2). `verify-ascent` asserts each kind is reachable rather than just counting the total, so the branch cannot go dead again.
