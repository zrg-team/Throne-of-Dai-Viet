# Commercial use of Vạn Thắng

**Short version.** The game is free, and stays free to play. You may fork it, learn
from it, mod it and share it, for anything that is not commercial. Anything
commercial is reserved to the maintainer, and the only commercial builds of
Vạn Thắng are the official ones published from this repository. If you want to do
something commercial with it, ask first — the answer is often yes.

The binding terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE) — the PolyForm Noncommercial License
1.0.0. This page is the plain-language policy that sits on top of it.

---

## What you may do without asking anyone

- **Play it**, on the web, or installed from the browser, or from the app stores.
- **Read the source**, and learn from it — that is a large part of why it is public.
- **Fork it, modify it, and run your fork**, for yourself or with friends.
- **Publish a fork or a mod** that is free, ad-free and not monetised, as long as
  it keeps the copyright notice, ships the licence with it, and is clear that it
  is not the official game.
- **Use it in teaching** — a classroom, a workshop, a university course, a museum
  or library exhibit, a student project. Schools and public institutions are
  explicitly a permitted purpose under the licence.
- **Stream it, record it, write about it, screenshot it.** Videos of you playing
  are yours, monetised or not; that is normal fair use of a game and nothing here
  is meant to restrict it.
- **Send a pull request** — see [Contributing](#contributing) below.

## What needs a licence from the maintainer

Anything commercial. Concretely, these all need written permission first:

- Selling the game, a fork, a mod, or a reskin of it — anywhere, at any price.
- Publishing it to a store under your own developer account, paid or free.
- Putting **advertising**, **in-app purchases**, subscriptions, a paywall, or any
  other monetisation into it or around it.
- Bundling it, or parts of it, into a commercial product, service or platform —
  including a games portal, an app that carries ads, or a paid compilation.
- Reusing pieces of it commercially on their own: the procedural Đông Hồ drawing
  code, the 277 portrait parts under `public/faces/`, the hero roster, the story
  templates, the map generator, the artwork produced by any of it.
- Selling merchandise using its artwork, name or branding.
- Training a commercial model on this repository, or shipping its content as part
  of a commercial dataset.
- Use inside a for-profit company for that company's benefit, beyond an individual
  reading the code or experimenting privately.

If you are not sure which side of the line you are on, ask. Asking costs nothing
and the answer is quick.

## Why it is set up this way

The game is free and ad-free by choice, and it should stay that way for the person
holding the phone. Reserving the commercial rights is what keeps that promise
holdable: it means nobody can take this work, wrap it in advertising or a price
tag, and ship it to Vietnamese players as though it were the real thing. There is
one official Vạn Thắng, it comes from this repository, and it does not ask anyone
for money.

It also matters that the history is told carefully. A commercial fork made in a
hurry, by someone with no stake in getting Trần Quốc Toản or Bạch Đằng right, does
damage that is hard to undo. Keeping the commercial channel single and named keeps
that responsibility somewhere.

None of that requires restricting the things that make a project worth publishing —
reading it, learning from it, modding it, teaching with it. Those stay open.

## How to ask

Open an issue in this repository titled **"Commercial licence request"**, or reach
the maintainer through [github.com/zrg-team](https://github.com/zrg-team).

Say in a few lines:

1. **Who you are** — person, studio, school, publisher.
2. **What you want to do**, concretely.
3. **Which parts** you need — the whole game, or just the art, the code, the data.
4. **How it would be monetised**, if it would be.
5. **Where it would be published**, and for which audience.

Commercial licences are granted case by case, in writing. Terms depend on what is
being asked for. Requests from Vietnamese schools, museums, cultural institutions
and non-profits are welcome and will be treated generously — in most such cases the
existing noncommercial licence already covers what you need, and no request is
necessary at all.

Permission granted for one thing does not carry over to another. A licence for a
store listing is not a licence for merchandise.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome, and there is one thing to know before you send one.

Because commercial rights are reserved, **by opening a pull request you license
your contribution under the same terms as the rest of the project, and grant the
maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free right to use, modify,
relicense and commercially exploit it as part of Vạn Thắng** — including in the
official store-published builds. You keep the copyright in what you wrote; this is
the permission that lets your work ship inside the official game.

If you cannot or would rather not grant that, please say so in the pull request —
it will not be merged, but an issue describing the change is still genuinely
useful, and often the faster route anyway.

Do not paste code into this repository that you do not have the right to license
this way — including code copied from projects under a different licence.
