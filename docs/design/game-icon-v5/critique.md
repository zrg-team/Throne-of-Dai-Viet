# Đông Hồ mobile icon comparison

## Reference observations

The user's three reference prints share confident irregular black contours, broad flat blocks of green, red and yellow, and faces expressed with very few marks. Their subjects have personality and action. The pig print is especially useful for the strong silhouette and balanced large decorative areas; its animal is not being copied into this game's icon.

The images establish the requested visual treatment. They are not being used as evidence of a named ruler's lifetime likeness or a particular dynasty's uniform. No source text is copied into the icon.

## Refinement review

The final C removes the top and bottom fringe and the dangling tie, shortens the staff, and provides a substantially calmer outline. Four broad trailing points remain instead of the requested three; their shape remains legible, so no further cosmetic iteration was needed. The final has faint paper tone and texture despite the request for a uniform background. The adaptive export's paper padding is nearly outside the visible launcher viewport and is unobtrusive in the crop preview.

At 64 px, the flag and center sun remain distinct. At 32 px, the fine texture disappears while the silhouette and color blocks remain. The result sacrifices some of the first flag's lively flourish to meet the user's request for a simpler mobile icon. It also has less emotional personality than the commander; that remains the main tradeoff.
