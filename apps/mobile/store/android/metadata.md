### Full description  _(1704/4000)_

```
One castle. One small army. One ruler with more ambition than land.

Vạn Thắng is a grand strategy game drawn in the ink of Đông Hồ woodblock prints — the folk art of northern Vietnam, cut by hand and printed on shell-coated paper. Every hex of the map, every hero's face, every banner is drawn in that tradition rather than rendered.

You begin as a minor lord on a Vietnam-inspired map. A farm answers hunger. An iron mountain arms your infantry. A port pays for the next campaign. A temple steadies a court that would otherwise turn on you. Every neighbouring land is a visible want, and every one of them belongs to somebody.

FOUR WAYS TO PLAY

• Conquest — take the map one province at a time, by war, marriage or coin
• Throne of Empires — an endless reign, with prestige and a dynasty that outlives you
• Dragon Ascent — a roguelite of endless waves; pause, pick a card, and hold the line
• Battle Lab — the combat system alone, to learn its edges

WAR IS A CONVERSATION

Formations answer formations. Terrain answers both. A general's temper decides what he does with an order you gave an hour ago. Battles resolve in three breaths, and you may take command of any of them or let your marshals handle it.

A COURT THAT REMEMBERS

Heroes are people, not stat blocks — 125 of them drawn from real Vietnamese history, each with a life before you found them. Ministers argue. Generals sulk. The chronicle writes down what actually happened, including the parts you would rather it forgot.

NO INTERNET. NO ACCOUNT. NO ADS.

The entire game runs on your device. There is no login, no server, no telemetry, and nothing to buy. Turn off the network and it plays exactly the same.

Free and open source.
```
