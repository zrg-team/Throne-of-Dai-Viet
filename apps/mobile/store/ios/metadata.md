### Description  _(2946/4000)_

```
THE DRUM IS ALREADY FALLING. THEY ARE ALREADY COMING.

One citadel. A handful of spearmen. A country that will not wait for you to be ready.

Take a province — the next invasion comes harder.
Draft a card — the one after that comes harder still.
Everything you win sharpens the blade pointed at you.

There is no victory screen. Only how far you got.

READ THE FIELD OR BLEED FOR IT

Their spears are set. Your line is still forming. The drum falls in five.

Five shapes, each the answer to another — the hedge, the wedge, the swarm, the tortoise, the volley. The enemy tells you what they hold. You have seconds to answer it.

Lean the right way and their line folds. Lean the wrong way and you watch four thousand men become two.

Press harder to win faster and spend them faster. Wager everything on the shape you already hold. Commit the reserve — you only get to do it once.

Or hand the whole battle to your generals, and live with what they decide.

THREE ANSWER THE CALL. ONE WILL SERVE.

Summon champions out of a thousand years of Vietnamese history. Yết Kiêu the diver. Trần Quốc Toản, too young for the council. Lê Lai, who wore another man's coat and did not come back.

They are not portraits in a menu. They govern your provinces. They lead your armies — and an army with no one at its head fights like it.

They also have lives you did not ask for. Rivals start eating together. A loyal general asks for more than you can give. Someone you paid for years walks out the gate.

THE LEGENDS COME LOOKING FOR YOU

The reed banners. The divine crossbow and the goose feathers. The stakes hidden under the Bạch Đằng. The elders at Diên Hồng who answered with one word.

These are not cutscenes you skip. A story arrives holding the general you actually summoned, and it spends your actual gold and your actual men. Some ask you to swear something — and remember, later, whether you kept it.

NOTHING HERE IS A SPRITE

Every hex, every face, every banner is drawn in the manner of a Đông Hồ woodblock print — the folk art of northern Vietnam, cut by hand onto shell-coated paper.

The paddies flood, ripen and are cut. Winter strips the trees. Your armies stand in the shape they are actually holding, under their own colours, with their camps behind them.

You will be able to read a battle at arm's length.

FOUR WAYS IN

Dragon Ascent — endless waves, cards, and one run that goes as far as you can push it.
Throne of Empires — a reign that outlives you, and a dynasty that inherits it.
Campaign — take the country province by province, by war, marriage or coin.
Skirmish — one fight, nothing else. The quickest way to learn how battles work.

BUY IT ONCE

No ads. No energy timers. No season pass. No premium currency. Nothing waiting behind another payment, ever.

AND IT NEVER PHONES HOME

No login. No account. No servers. Switch off the network on a plane and it plays exactly the same.

Đại Việt is waiting. The drum is falling.
```
